## Objective

Build the patient-facing features including registration, profile management, and medical records access.

## Tasks

1. Patient registration with demographics
2. Profile management (update, avatar, preferences)
3. Medical history view
4. Document upload and management
5. Consent management

## Constraints

- Patients can only access their own data
- Medical records are append-only (immutable once finalized)
- All data access logged for HIPAA compliance
- Minors require guardian registration
