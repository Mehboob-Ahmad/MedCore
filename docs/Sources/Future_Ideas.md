# 💡 Future Ideas

> A living document of potential features, innovations, and expansion opportunities for MedicHp.

---

## Clinical Features
- AI-powered triage system
- Wearable device data integration (Apple Health, Google Fit)
- Remote patient monitoring (RPM)
- E-prescriptions with pharmacy integration
- Genetic health risk assessments

## Platform Expansion
- Marketplace for health services
- White-label solution for clinics and hospitals
- API-as-a-product for third-party integrations
- Partner ecosystem (labs, pharmacies, insurance)

## Technology Innovations
- Voice-enabled appointment booking
- AR/VR for medical education and patient guidance
- Blockchain for medical record integrity
- Edge computing for offline-first mobile experience

## Business Models
- SaaS subscription tiers (Free, Pro, Enterprise)
- Per-appointment transaction fees
- Premium telemedicine features
- Analytics and insights packages for healthcare providers

---

> **Note:** This is a brainstorming document. Items here are not committed and require proper evaluation via ADRs before implementation.
